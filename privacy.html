Privacy policy

Drive MCP Server is a personal tool used only by its owner, Hussein Khafadji, with his own Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads, creates and edits files in the owner's Google Drive so that his Claude Science workspace can work with them.

How data is handled

  • Google sign-in credentials are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • File contents are passed to the owner's Claude Science session (Anthropic's Claude) when he asks for them, and are handled under his own Anthropic account. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • No other users' data is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
